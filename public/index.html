The route

The orders decide where the car goes

No timetable to keep. The app works out the route from the orders waiting, and changes it every time a new one comes in.

📍 One area at a time

  • The app clears every order in an area before moving on, so you're not zig-zagging across town. A new order near where you already are gets slotted straight in.

💬 Sales along the way

  • When the route passes through a neighbourhood, people there who opted in get one text about 5 minutes ahead. Ordering from a car that's already coming down your street is an easy yes.

⏱️ Honest times

  • Customers see where the car is heading next and a live estimate to their door. We never say "Fox Mountain at 9:30", because orders change the plan. If it's busy, they see the longer wait before they pay.

🚗 No orders? Cruise

  • When the list is empty, the app suggests the busiest spot for that hour, and customers see "Cruising Downtown". The first order of the next wave sets the route again.
Who does what

Three people, three jobs

🏁 You, the driver

  • 🛒Buy the stock (Costco or wholesale) and load the car. It's your inventory.
  • 🚗Go where the orders are. The app plans your stops; you drive and hand off at front doors.
  • 💵Keep the sales. During the trial, 100% of it.
  • 📣Hustle locally: posters, word of mouth, being the friendly face.

💻 Oran, Stockcar

  • 📱Runs the apps: ordering, payments, your driver screen, the area texts.
  • 🎨Brand and car vinyl, plus help with marketing and socials.
  • 📊Tracks the numbers so we both know if it's working.
  • 🆓Free for the trial month. The ongoing deal gets agreed before you start.

📱 The customer

  • 🧃Mostly teens and young people without cars, wanting snacks after school and at night.
  • 🏠Plus anyone who doesn't want to drive for a pop and chips.
  • 💳Pays up front in the app. You never handle cash.
  • 💬Opts in to get one text when the car is passing near them.

The deal and the prep

Trial terms and before day one

📝 Trial month terms

🗓️One month, evenings 3–11 PM. You pick which nights.
💵You keep 100% of sales and delivery fees. The software is free.
🎨Oran covers the app, the car vinyl, and marketing help.
💳Card and Apple Pay only, paid in the app before you drive. No cash.
🤝After the trial, if it works: the software is ___% of sales or $___/month. We fill this in together before day one, so nobody's surprised later.

✅ Before day one

  • Business licence: call Williams Lake City Hall about a delivery-only business with no storefront.
  • Car insurance for delivery use: call an Autoplan broker. Delivering on a personal-use policy can leave you personally on the hook after a crash.
  • Powered cooler for drinks, plugged into the car. Pop freezes in a cold car and chocolate melts in a hot one.
  • Phone mount and car charger.
  • Starting stock: roughly $500–800 at Costco or a wholesaler, 40–60 products.
  • Payout account set up so card payments land in your bank.
  • Tax check with an accountant. Small sellers may not need to charge GST or PST during a short trial.
