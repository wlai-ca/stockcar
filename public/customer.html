The customer side

The corner store is a car.

This is what people in Williams Lake would see. Everything for sale is already in the car, so delivery starts the moment they order. There's no fixed schedule: the car goes where the orders are, evenings 3–11 PM, and the app shows where it's heading next.

  • 🍫Menu: add a few snacks. It's only what's in the car tonight.
  • 🛒Cart: flat $3 delivery, free over $25. Pay with Apple Pay (pretend).
  • 📍Order: watch the car drive to your door.
  • 💬A few seconds in, you'll get the "passing near you" text customers opt in to.
Try the customer app →

It opens full screen. Nothing is real and no money moves, so tap anything. To get back here, use your browser's back button.
